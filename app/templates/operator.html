Service Context · Beta · Optional

Test this before using it in a live service

Service Context uses deterministic temporary corrections by default, so uploaded suggestions do not change Whisper's recognition or normal caption workload.

Experimental option: Direct recognition assistance may increase processing load, caption latency, or unstable wording on some computers and speech models. It is off by default, limited to two selected terms, and should be tested before production use. A full script does not become a word-for-word source—the microphone and Whisper still decide every caption.

Prefer an explicit “heard as → replace with” correction after a short rehearsal. Enable recognition assistance only for a particularly difficult name, then compare accuracy and delay with it switched off. Bible-reference formatting remains available without uploaded notes.
